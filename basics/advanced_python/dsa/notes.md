## Time Complexity
The Big `O` notation in mathematics refer to the upper bound of a function. <br> 
In computer science, the big `O` notation refers to the worst case number of operations needed by the algorithm. <br>

<b>Definition</b>: <br>
Let `f(n)` & `g(n)` be 2 functions. Then `f(n)` is `O(g(n))` if and only if there are positive constants `C` & <b>n<sub>0</sub></b>, such that, <br>
`C.g(n) > f(n)` for all <b> n > n<sub>0</sub> </b>. <br>
